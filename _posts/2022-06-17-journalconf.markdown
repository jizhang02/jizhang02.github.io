## Publishers & Platforms


### Fully open access publishers

- [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/) — **Fully OA**; multidisciplinary journals.
- [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/about/open-access) — **Fully OA**; includes biomedical and health sciences.
- [PLOS](https://plos.org/) — **Fully OA**; includes PLOS ONE, PLOS Medicine, and PLOS Biology.
- [JMIR Publications](https://www.jmirpublications.com/) — **Fully OA**; focuses on digital health and medical informatics.

### Publishers with both fully OA and subscription / hybrid journals

- [Elsevier](https://www.elsevier.com/) — **Fully OA + subscription / hybrid**; publishes Medical Image Analysis and Computers in Biology and Medicine.
- [Springer Nature](https://www.springernature.com/gp/open-science/journals-books/journals) — **Fully OA + subscription / hybrid**; includes Nature Portfolio, Springer, and BMC.
- [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/) — **Fully OA + subscription / hybrid**; publishes Medical Physics on behalf of AAPM.
- [IOP Publishing](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/open_access/) — **Fully OA + subscription / hybrid**; publishes Physics in Medicine & Biology.
- [Taylor & Francis](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/choose-open/) — **Fully OA + subscription / hybrid**; publishes Computer Methods in Biomechanics and Biomedical Engineering.
- [IEEE](https://open.ieee.org/) — **Fully OA + subscription / hybrid**; a professional association and publisher of TMI, TBME, JBHI, and TRPMS.

**OA (Open Access)** means articles are openly available to readers. A fully OA journal makes all its articles open; a hybrid journal combines subscription articles with articles published OA. OA articles are free to read, but authors may need to pay a publication fee. Policies and fees should be checked on the individual journal website.
